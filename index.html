Born from a Vision of Beauty

LUFOSCA was founded with one belief: that handmade jewelry should feel as luxurious as fine jewelry from any boutique in the world.

Every collection is designed around a feeling — the warmth of pearls at dusk, the coolness of crystals under moonlight.

✦ Our Policies

Shipping & Returns

Shipping Policy

LUFOSCA ships across Pakistan via trusted courier services. Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days of confirmation.

Free Shipping on all orders over Rs. 3,000. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days.

All orders are confirmed via WhatsApp before dispatch.

Returns Policy

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery — provided items are unworn, undamaged, and in original packaging.

Custom and personalized orders are non-refundable. Sale items may only be exchanged.

Exchanges

Exchanges are processed within 3–5 days of receiving your returned item.

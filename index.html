✦ Our Policies

Shipping & Returns

Shipping Policy

LUFOSCA ships across Pakistan via trusted courier services. Orders dispatched within 1–2 business days.

Free Shipping on all orders over Rs. 3,000. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days.

Returns Policy

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery — provided items are unworn, undamaged, and in original packaging.

Custom orders are non-refundable. Sale items may only be exchanged.

Exchanges

Exchanges are processed within 3–5 days. Shipping for the first exchange per order is covered by LUFOSCA.

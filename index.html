Crafted Whispers

From the first bead to the final clasp, LUFOSCA is a love letter to craftsmanship, femininity, and timeless beauty.

✦ Our Policies

Shipping & Returns

Shipping Policy

LUFOSCA ships across Pakistan via trusted courier services. Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days of confirmation.

Free Shipping on all orders over Rs. 3,000. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available at checkout for an additional fee.

All orders are confirmed via WhatsApp before dispatch. You will receive a tracking number once your package is on its way.

Returns Policy

We want you to love every LUFOSCA piece. If you're not satisfied, we accept returns within 7 days of delivery — provided items are unworn, undamaged, and in original packaging.

To initiate a return, contact us at hello@lufosca.com or DM us on Instagram with your order number and reason for return.

Custom and personalized orders are non-refundable. Sale items may only be exchanged.

Exchanges

Exchanges are processed within 3–5 days of receiving your returned item. Shipping costs for exchanges are covered by LUFOSCA on the first exchange per order.
